Immigration & border requirements

This section is based on current Nepal Immigration guidance available in August 2026. Re-check official guidance immediately before departure.

For Indian citizens: Nepal does not require a visa. For entry, the current Birgunj Immigration Office says Indian citizens should present a valid Indian passport or an Election Commission-issued voter ID. Aadhaar and driving licence should not be relied upon as entry documents.
Preferred

Indian Passport

Carry the original valid passport. This is the cleanest option for the group.

Alternative

Original Voter ID

Election Commission-issued voter identity card is the stated alternative for Indian citizens without passports.

Do not rely on

Aadhaar / Driving Licence

Current Birgunj immigration guidance says these are not accepted for entry.

Carry for every traveller

Border rule: Keep original IDs in your day bag, not inside checked/main luggage. At Raxaul/Birgunj, follow the immigration and customs instructions at the crossing. Do not assume that an app screenshot or a non-approved ID is enough.

Currency & emergency contacts

Indian currency rules in Nepal: ₹2,000 notes are not accepted at all. ₹500 and ₹200 notes issued after November 2016 are allowed only up to a limited total per person (₹25,000, as of the most recent rule change) — this has changed more than once, so re-check before you cross. Simplest approach: carry mostly ₹100 notes and smaller, and change the rest to Nepali rupees.
